This is actually a really cute project. Since you're already comfortable with web development, I'd make it feel like a private relationship OS instead of just a website.

## ❤️ Home Screen

* Countdown to your next meeting
* "X Days Left ❤️"
* Your two photos on opposite sides that slowly move closer every day.
* When the countdown reaches 0, both photos meet in the center with a heart animation and confetti.

Example:

```
👦 ---------------------- 👧
        48 Days Left

After 24 days:
👦 ----------- 👧

Meeting Day:
👦❤️👧
```

### ✨ A unique idea

Create a **"Virtual Hug"** feature.

Whenever one of you taps the ❤️ Hug button:

* The other gets a notification.
* The phone vibrates (where supported).
* An animation plays with your avatars hugging.
* A counter tracks:

  > "You have hugged each other 437 times."

It's a simple feature, but it adds a surprisingly personal touch to the app.
